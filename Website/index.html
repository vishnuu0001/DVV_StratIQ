Platform thesis

LLM-assisted where judgment matters. Deterministic where correctness matters.

Local-first AI

Most modules use local Ollama-backed inference and keep sensitive ticket, code, infrastructure, and assessment data inside the enterprise boundary.

Deterministic fallbacks

Scoring, classification, wave planning, formula math, and evidence validation remain reproducible when the LLM is unavailable.

Audit-ready outputs

Golden records, rationales, formula trails, retrieved evidence, and decision provenance are exposed instead of hidden behind opaque model text.
